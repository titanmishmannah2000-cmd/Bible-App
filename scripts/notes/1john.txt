1 | John declares what he has heard, seen and touched: the Word of life. “God is light, and in him is no darkness at all.” “If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous to forgive us.” || The opening echoes John 1. Real fellowship with God means walking in the light.
2 | “If anyone sins, we have a Counselor with the Father, Jesus Christ, the righteous.” Whoever says he knows God must keep his commands and love his brother. Don’t love the world. || Jesus is “the atoning sacrifice for our sins, and not for ours only, but also for the whole world.”
3 | “See how great a love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God!” We know love by this: he laid down his life for us. Love in deed and truth. || “When he is revealed, we will be like him; for we will see him just as he is.”
4 | Test the spirits: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ came in the flesh is from God. “God is love.” “There is no fear in love; but perfect love casts out fear.” “We love him, because he first loved us.” || The heart of the letter: love comes from God and shows itself in loving others.
5 | Faith in Jesus overcomes the world. “God gave to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.” “These things I have written… that you may know that you have eternal life.” || The words added to 5:7–8 in some later Bibles (the “Johannine Comma”) are not in the early Greek manuscripts.
