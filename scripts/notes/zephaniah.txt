1 | The great day of Yahweh is near, a day of wrath, distress and darkness, against Judah’s idolatry and complacency. || Baal was openly worshipped, and some swore by the Lord and by Milcom (Molech) at once [[tbd:002:Book of Zephaniah]]. Scripture condemned worship of the sun, moon and stars [[tosn:Zephaniah 1:5]]. It is “the day of Yahweh’s wrath” (1:15, 18), when God answers human sin [[tbd:002:Book of Zephaniah]].
2 | “Seek Yahweh, all you humble of the land.” Judgement on the Philistines, Moab, Ammon, Cush and Assyria. || After the terrifying scene Zephaniah urges the nation to repent and humble itself [[tosn:Zephaniah 2:1–3]]; true humility means depending on God [[tosn:Zephaniah 2:3]]. Nineveh had not yet fallen (2:13–15) [[tbd:002:Book of Zephaniah]].
3 | Jerusalem is rebuked, but God will purify the lips of the peoples, and leave a humble remnant. “Yahweh, your God, is among you, a mighty one who will save. He will rejoice over you with joy… He will rejoice over you with singing.” || The blessings promised to the humble remnant will reach people of every nation [[tosn:Zephaniah 3:9]]. The commands to sing and shout show the great joy God’s people will one day know [[tosn:Zephaniah 3:14]] [[tosn:Zephaniah 3:9–20]].
