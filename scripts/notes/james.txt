1 | Count it joy when you face trials; they produce endurance. Ask God for wisdom. Every good gift comes from above. “let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, and slow to anger” Be doers of the word, not hearers only. Pure religion: care for orphans and widows. || Many sayings echo the Sermon on the Mount.
2 | Don’t show favouritism to the rich. “Faith, if it has no works, is dead.” Abraham and Rahab showed their faith by what they did. || James and Paul use “works” differently: Paul means works of the law to earn standing; James means the deeds that genuine faith produces.
3 | The tongue is small but powerful, like a ship’s rudder or a spark that sets a forest ablaze. Wisdom from above is pure, peaceable and full of mercy. || “Let not many of you be teachers”
4 | Quarrels come from desires at war within you. “God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble.” “Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you.” Don’t boast about tomorrow: life is a vapour. || James quotes Proverbs 3:34, also quoted in 1 Peter 5:5.
5 | A warning to rich oppressors who withhold workers’ wages. Be patient like a farmer. “The prayer of faith will heal him who is sick.” Elijah was a man like us, and his prayer was powerful. || Anointing the sick with oil grew into church practices of prayer for healing.
