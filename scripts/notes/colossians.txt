1 | Paul prays for the Colossians and praises Christ: “the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation… all things have been created through him and for him… in him all things are held together.” || The Christ hymn (1:15–20) presents Christ as creator and reconciler of all things.
2 | In Christ “all the fullness of the Deity dwells bodily.” Don’t be taken captive by empty philosophy, rules about food and festivals, or the worship of angels. || The cross disarmed the powers and triumphed over them.
3 | “Set your mind on the things that are above.” Put off anger and lies; put on compassion, kindness and love. “Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly.” Household relationships. || In Christ there is no Greek or Jew, slave or free.
4 | Devote yourselves to prayer; let your speech be gracious. Greetings, including from Luke, “the beloved physician”, and Mark. || A letter to Laodicea is mentioned (4:16), now lost or perhaps Ephesians.
