1 | A great hymn of praise: God chose us in Christ before the world was made, adopted us, redeemed us, and plans to sum up all things in Christ. Paul prays they will know the hope of God’s calling. || 1:3–14 is a single long sentence in Greek. The Spirit is the guarantee of our inheritance.
2 | We were dead in sin, but God made us alive with Christ: “By grace you have been saved through faith… not of works.” Christ has broken down the wall between Jews and Gentiles, making one new people. || 2:8–10 links grace and good works: “We are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works.”
3 | The mystery revealed to Paul: the Gentiles are fellow heirs. Paul prays that they may know “the width and length and height and depth” of Christ’s love. || “Now to him who is able to do exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think.”
4 | Walk worthy of your calling: one body, one Spirit, one Lord, one faith, one baptism. Christ gave gifts to equip the church. Put off the old self; speak truth in love; don’t let the sun go down on your anger. || Paul quotes Psalm 68:18 about Christ’s ascension.
5 | Be imitators of God; walk in love and light; be filled with the Spirit, singing psalms and hymns. Wives and husbands: “Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the assembly, and gave himself up for it.” || The household code begins with “subjecting yourselves to one another”. Marriage pictures Christ and the church.
6 | Children and parents, servants and masters. “Put on the whole armour of God”: the belt of truth, the breastplate of righteousness, the shield of faith, the sword of the Spirit. || The armour draws on Isaiah 59. “Our wrestling is not against flesh and blood.”
