1 | Paul tells Titus to appoint elders in every town on Crete: blameless, hospitable, holding firmly to the truth. || Paul quotes a Cretan poet’s joke about Cretans, a sign of his knowledge of Greek literature.
2 | Teach older and younger men and women, and slaves, to live well. “The grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all men,” training us to live godly lives as we wait for “our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ.” || 2:13 is one of the clearest New Testament statements calling Jesus God.
3 | Be ready for every good work. God saved us “not by works of righteousness… but according to his mercy, he saved us through the washing of regeneration and renewing by the Holy Spirit.” || Another compact summary of the gospel (3:4–7).
