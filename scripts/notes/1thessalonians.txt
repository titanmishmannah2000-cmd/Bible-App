1 | Paul remembers the Thessalonians’ “work of faith and labour of love and perseverance of hope”: they turned from idols to serve the living God. || Faith, hope and love together, as in 1 Corinthians 13.
2 | Paul recalls his gentle ministry among them, “like a nursing mother”, and their suffering for the gospel. || Paul worked night and day so as not to be a burden.
3 | Timothy brings good news of their faith, and Paul prays they will abound in love. || Paul’s deep affection for a young church.
4 | Live holy lives and work quietly. Those who have died in Christ will rise first when the Lord comes, “so we will be with the Lord forever. Therefore comfort one another.” || 4:13–18 is the main source for teaching on the “rapture”; its purpose is comfort for the grieving.
5 | The day of the Lord comes like a thief, so stay awake. “Always rejoice. Pray without ceasing. In everything give thanks.” || Short commands for daily Christian life close the letter.
