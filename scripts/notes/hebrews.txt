1 | God spoke in many ways through the prophets, but now through his Son, the radiance of his glory, who made purification for sins and sat down at God’s right hand. The Son is greater than the angels. || A chain of seven Old Testament quotations, mostly from the Psalms.
2 | Don’t drift away. Jesus was made a little lower than the angels and shared our flesh and blood, to destroy the power of death and help those who are tempted. || Psalm 8 is applied to Jesus. He is not ashamed to call us brothers.
3 | Jesus is greater than Moses: a son over God’s house, not a servant in it. “Today if you will hear his voice, don’t harden your hearts.” || The author preaches on Psalm 95.
4 | A Sabbath rest remains for God’s people. “The word of God is living and active, and sharper than any two-edged sword.” We have a great high priest who sympathizes with our weaknesses, so approach the throne of grace boldly. || 4:15: Jesus was tempted in every way, yet without sin.
5 | Every high priest is chosen from among men; Christ was appointed by God, “a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek”. He learned obedience through suffering. || Psalm 110:4 becomes the centre of the argument.
6 | Move on to maturity. A severe warning about falling away, then encouragement: God’s promise and oath are an anchor for the soul. || 6:4–6 is one of the most debated passages on whether believers can lose their salvation.
7 | Melchizedek, king of righteousness and peace, was greater than Abraham. Jesus is a priest forever, who “he lives forever to make intercession” for us. || The argument draws on Genesis 14 and Psalm 110.
8 | Jesus serves in the true tabernacle in heaven and mediates a better covenant. Jeremiah’s new covenant is quoted in full. || The longest Old Testament quotation in the New (Jeremiah 31:31–34).
9 | The earthly tabernacle and the Day of Atonement were a shadow. Christ entered heaven itself “through his own blood… having obtained eternal redemption.” || “apart from shedding of blood there is no remission.” Christ appeared once at the end of the ages.
10 | The law’s sacrifices could never take away sins, but Christ offered one sacrifice for all time. “let’s draw near… let’s hold fast… Let’s consider how to provoke one another to love and good works,” not forsaking meeting together. || Psalm 40 is placed on Christ’s lips.
11 | “Faith is assurance of things hoped for, proof of things not seen.” The great roll call of faith: Abel, Enoch, Noah, Abraham, Sarah, Moses, Rahab and many more. || The Old Testament retold as one story of trusting God for a promised future.
12 | “let’s run with perseverance the race that is set before us, looking to Jesus, the author and perfecter of faith” God disciplines his children. You have come to Mount Zion, the city of the living God. || The contrast between Sinai and Zion.
13 | Keep loving one another; show hospitality, “for in doing so, some have entertained angels without knowing it.” “Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever.” || The benediction calls Jesus “the great shepherd of the sheep”.
