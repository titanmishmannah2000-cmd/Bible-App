1 | Paul praises their growing faith under persecution and assures them God will set things right when Jesus is revealed. || God’s justice is a comfort for the suffering.
2 | The day of the Lord has not yet come; first “the man of sin” must be revealed. Stand firm in the teaching you received. || Identifying the man of lawlessness and “the restrainer” is much debated.
3 | Pray for us. Keep away from those who refuse to work: “If anyone is not willing to work, don’t let him eat.” “don’t be weary in doing what is right.” || Paul signs with his own hand, a mark of authenticity.
